## Information we collect
- Account info: name, phone number, email (optional), password (stored hashed, never in plain text)
- Delivery addresses and, if you enable location services, precise location to improve delivery accuracy
- Order history and payment method selection (Cash on Delivery / Card on Delivery / Wallet — we do not store card numbers; card payments are processed by [PAYMENT PROCESSOR] if/when enabled)
- Device and usage data (app version, crash logs) for reliability and security

## How we use it
- To process and deliver your orders
- To communicate order status (push notifications, SMS)
- To improve the app and detect fraud/abuse
- We do not sell your personal data to third parties

## Sharing
- With the vendor (restaurant/market) fulfilling your order — name, phone, delivery address, order items
- With delivery riders — name, phone, delivery address
- With payment processors, only if you use card/wallet payment methods
- With law enforcement only where legally required

## Your rights
- Access, correct, or delete your account data by contacting [SUPPORT EMAIL]
- Opt out of promotional notifications in-app under Profile → Notifications

## Data retention
- Account and order data retained while your account is active, and for [X years] afterward for tax/legal record-keeping, per Egyptian commercial record requirements

## Children
- Delifast is not directed at children under 16; we do not knowingly collect data from them

## Changes to this policy
- We'll notify users in-app of material changes before they take effect
